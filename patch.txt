DJControl Mix Ultra patch groups
================================

All offsets are file offsets. Add 0x08000000 for the flash address.

1. Jog ring gates (default)
---------------------------
0x00608D  D9 -> E0   left ring gate
0x0060B3  D9 -> E0   right ring gate

The Thumb instruction at each address changes from `bls` to `b`, so slow
encoder edges take the send path instead of being dropped.

2. Pitch fader dead zones
-------------------------
0x00B75A  80 -> 40   left pitch fader
0x00B75B  80 -> 40   right pitch fader

These are entries 4 and 5 of the 14-byte dead-zone table at 0x0800B756.
The pitch faders send 14-bit CC8 (MSB) plus CC40 (LSB).

3. Dead-zone calibration for the other controls
-----------------------------------------------
0x00B756  80 -> 60   crossfader
0x00B757  80 -> 60   master
0x00B758  80 -> 60   channel volume 1
0x00B759  80 -> 60   channel volume 2
0x00B75C  80 -> 60   filter 1
0x00B75D  80 -> 60   filter 2
0x00B75E  80 -> 60   low EQ 1
0x00B75F  80 -> 60   low EQ 2
0x00B760  80 -> 60   mid EQ 1
0x00B761  80 -> 60   mid EQ 2
0x00B762  80 -> 60   high EQ 1
0x00B763  80 -> 60   high EQ 2

The pitch entries at 0x00B75A/B are intentionally excluded from this group, so
the two groups can be selected independently.
